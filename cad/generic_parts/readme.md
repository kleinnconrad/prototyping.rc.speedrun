# Generic parts
The files in this directory are the drive line, the pivot blocks, the wishbones and the running gear of the speedrun chassis, the owner's own design. Purchased parts (owner, 2026-11-24): camber links, cup joints of the centre driveshaft, CVD drive shafts, diff outdrives, inner hinge pins of the wishbones, steering links, servo link and servo horn, and sway bars with holders, ball ends and drop links. They are modelled in memory for the assembly, its picture and the checks only and have no files here. Some of the own parts are based on parts of the Carten T410R (for example the interfaces: bearings, gears, screws), others deviate strongly from them. The parts designed for the speedrun chassis (chassis plate, bulkheads, top brace, shock towers, front splitter, battery tray) are stored in `cad/speedrun_parts` (see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#speedrun-chassis)).

Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (test print or on the car), $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification, $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified. Purchased parts (assembly only, no part files) have no status. The parts marked red are first designs: they still need a lot of refinement (dimensions, strength, fit and function on the car) and are not ready for manufacturing.

* **`/pivot_block`**: Pivot blocks of the wishbones, long block (1 piece) and short block (3 pieces), STEP, STL and parameter file. Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (3D printed prototype, bores 5.0 mm for the hinge pins). Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#pivot-blocks).
* **`/driveshaft`**: Centre driveshaft between the pinions of the two bulkheads, with the fixed metal pins, STEP and STL; parameter file of all drive line parts. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification. The cup joints are purchased (assembly only).
* **`/rear_drive`**: Rear pinion shaft with flange, pinion 17T, spur gear 72T 48DP, bearing 5x10x4 and retaining pin of the cup joint, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification.
* **`/motor_mount`**: Motor mount for 540 motors and its cap as the original part (photos of the owner), with longer long holes of the motor screws, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified with a test print (technically good; cosmetic changes after the print: smooth faces, round upper inner corner (R9), edge chamfers 0.3 mm on the motor mount except the bottom face).
* **`/front_drive`**: Front pinion shaft (End A), STEP and STL; pinion, bearings and retaining pin as in `/rear_drive`. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification.
* **`/diff`**: Differential (End B): case, ring gear 42T with hub and hexagon bore, side gears 14T, spider gears 9T, cross pin, shims and bearing 10x15x4; spool (End A) with the same ring gear, bearings and outdrives; STEP and STL. Fits the bearing seats, the case cavity and the ring gear recess of the bulkhead and meshes with the pinion 17T. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification. Description: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#differentials).
* **`/wishbone`**: Front and rear wishbones (lower suspension arms, 2 pieces each), STEP, STL and parameter file, generated with `scripts/cad/generate_wishbones.py`; [technical drawings](wishbone/readme.md). Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified. The inner hinge pins 3x42 are purchased (assembly only). Description, checks and the list of the measurements: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#wishbones).
* **`/front_corner`**: C-hubs with 10 degrees caster (right and left part), steering knuckles, axles with joint cup and hex hubs 12 mm at End A, STEP and STL; measurement sheet of the steering knuckle; [technical drawings](front_corner/readme.md) of the C-hubs and the steering knuckle; [kingpin sleeves and washers to buy](front_corner/readme.md#kingpin-of-the-c-hub-sleeves-and-washers-to-buy) with an explanation picture. Status: C-hubs and steering knuckles $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (3D prints of the owner; C-hubs: fit checked, 2026-11-24); axles and hex hubs $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/rear_corner`**: Rear hub carriers, axles with joint cup and hex hubs 12 mm at End B, STEP and STL. Status: hub carriers $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (3D print of the owner, fit checked, 2026-11-24); axles and hex hubs $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/steering`**: Steering posts, bellcranks and steering bridge, STEP and STL; [technical drawings](steering/readme.md) of the steering post, the bellcrank and the steering bridge; [bridge joint: sleeves and washers to buy](steering/readme.md#bridge-joint-sleeves-and-washers-to-buy) with an explanation picture. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification. The steering links, the servo link and the servo horn are purchased (assembly only).
* **`/servo_mount`**: Floating servo mount (top plate, fixed post, floating post), STEP and STL; [technical drawings](servo_mount/readme.md). Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (owner); the holes for the servo ears are to be checked against the servo before printing.
* **`running_gear_parameters.json`**: All dimensions of the running gear (including the purchased CVD drive shafts and sway bars), each with its source and a note. The parts of `/front_corner`, `/rear_corner`, `/steering` and `/servo_mount` are generated with `scripts/cad/generate_running_gear.py`.
* **`camber_link_parameters.json`**: Dimensions of the simplified model of the camber links (purchased parts: turnbuckle M3x37, rod ends, ball studs), used by `scripts/cad/generate_camber_links.py` and the running gear for the assembly; no part files. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#running-gear).
* **`parts_measurement_sheet.pdf`**: Measurement sheet (A4, 4 pages) of the dimensions that are not measured yet: front and rear wishbone (letters A to T on drawings, table with the model values and empty fields for the measured values) and the bearing seat of the motor mount (MM1 to MM8). Generated with `scripts/cad/generate_measurement_sheets.py`.
* The parts of `/driveshaft`, `/rear_drive`, `/front_drive`, `/motor_mount` and `/diff` are generated with `scripts/cad/generate_driveshaft.py` (the purchased cup joints and diff outdrives: assembly only). Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#centre-driveshaft-and-drive-line).
