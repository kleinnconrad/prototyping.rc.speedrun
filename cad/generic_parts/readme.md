# Generic parts
The files in this directory are the drive line, the pivot blocks, the wishbones, the running gear and the camber links of the speedrun chassis. The drive line, the pivot blocks, the wishbones and the running gear are the owner's own design; the camber links are purchased parts (simplified model for the assembly). Some of the own parts are based on parts of the Carten T410R (for example the interfaces: bearings, gears, screws), others deviate strongly from them. The parts designed for the speedrun chassis (chassis plate, bulkheads, top brace, shock towers, front splitter, battery tray) are stored in `cad/speedrun_parts` (see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#speedrun-chassis)).

Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (test print or on the car), $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification, $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified. The parts marked red are first designs: they still need a lot of refinement (dimensions, strength, fit and function on the car) and are not ready for manufacturing.

* **`/pivot_block`**: Pivot blocks of the wishbones, long block (1 piece) and short block (3 pieces), STEP, STL and parameter file. Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (3D printed prototype, bores 5.0 mm for the hinge pins). Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#pivot-blocks).
* **`/driveshaft`**: Centre driveshaft between the pinions of the two bulkheads, with the fixed metal pins, and the cup joints, STEP and STL; parameter file of all drive line parts. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification (centre driveshaft); cup joints: $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/rear_drive`**: Rear pinion shaft with flange, pinion 17T, spur gear 72T 48DP, bearing 5x10x4 and retaining pin of the cup joint, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification.
* **`/motor_mount`**: Motor mount for 540 motors and its cap as the original part (photos of the owner), with longer long holes of the motor screws, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified with a test print (technically good; cosmetic changes after the print: smooth faces, round upper inner corner (R9)).
* **`/camber_link`**: Camber links (4 pieces, purchased parts): simplified model for the assembly with the turnbuckle M3x37, rod end, ball stud and the assembled front and rear link, STEP, STL and parameter file, generated with `scripts/cad/generate_camber_links.py`.
* **`/front_drive`**: Front pinion shaft (End A), STEP and STL; pinion, bearings and retaining pin as in `/rear_drive`. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification.
* **`/diff`**: Differential (End B): case, ring gear 42T with hub and hexagon bore, side gears 14T, spider gears 9T, cross pin, shims and bearing 10x15x4; spool (End A) with the same ring gear, bearings and outdrives; STEP and STL. Fits the bearing seats, the case cavity and the ring gear recess of the bulkhead and meshes with the pinion 17T. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification. Description: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#differentials).
* **`/diff_outdrive`**: Diff outdrive with the slots for the holding pin of the dogbones, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/wishbone`**: Front and rear wishbones (lower suspension arms, 2 pieces each) and the inner hinge pin 3x42 (4 pieces), STEP, STL and parameter file, generated with `scripts/cad/generate_wishbones.py`. Status: $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification (wishbones); hinge pin: $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified. Description, checks and the list of the measurements: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#wishbones).
* **`/front_corner`**: C-hubs with 10 degrees caster (right and left part), steering knuckles, axles with joint cup and hex hubs 12 mm at End A, STEP and STL; measurement sheet of the steering knuckle; [technical drawings](front_corner/readme.md) of the C-hubs and the steering knuckle. Status: steering knuckles $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (3D print of the owner); C-hubs, axles and hex hubs $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified (C-hubs: 3D print verification with the guide sleeves planned by the owner).
* **`/rear_corner`**: Rear hub carriers, axles with joint cup and hex hubs 12 mm at End B, STEP and STL. Status: hub carriers $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification; axles and hex hubs $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/cvd_drive_shafts`**: CVD drive shafts at End A and End B, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/steering`**: Steering posts, bellcranks, steering bridge, steering links, servo link and servo horn, STEP and STL; [technical drawings](steering/readme.md) of the steering post, the bellcrank and the steering bridge. Status: steering posts, bellcranks and steering bridge $\color{gold}\blacktriangle$ measures qualified, no 3D print verification; links and servo horn $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`/servo_mount`**: Floating servo mount (top plate, fixed post, floating post), STEP and STL; [technical drawings](servo_mount/readme.md). Status: $\color{green}\checkmark$ verified (owner); the holes for the servo ears are to be checked against the servo before printing.
* **`/sway_bars`**: Sway bars with holders, ball ends and drop links at End A and End B, STEP and STL. Status: $\color{red}\times$ completely unqualified.
* **`running_gear_parameters.json`**: All dimensions of the running gear, each with its source and a note. The parts of `/front_corner`, `/rear_corner`, `/cvd_drive_shafts`, `/steering`, `/servo_mount` and `/sway_bars` are generated with `scripts/cad/generate_running_gear.py`. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#running-gear).
* **`parts_measurement_sheet.pdf`**: Measurement sheet (A4, 4 pages) of the dimensions that are not measured yet: front and rear wishbone (letters A to T on drawings, table with the model values and empty fields for the measured values) and the bearing seat of the motor mount (MM1 to MM8). Generated with `scripts/cad/generate_measurement_sheets.py`.
* The parts of `/driveshaft`, `/rear_drive`, `/motor_mount` and `/diff_outdrive` are generated with `scripts/cad/generate_driveshaft.py`. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#centre-driveshaft-and-drive-line).
