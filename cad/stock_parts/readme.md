# Stock parts of the Carten T410R
The files in this directory are models of stock parts of the Carten T410R. They are not part of the speedrun design and are not manufactured in this project. They are modelled for the completeness of the build: the assembly of the speedrun chassis contains them, so that the space around the speedrun parts can be checked.

* **`/pivot_block`**: Pivot blocks of the wishbones, long block (1 piece) and short block (3 pieces), STEP, STL and parameter file. Status: not verified. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#pivot-blocks-stock-parts-not-part-of-the-speedrun-design).
* **`/driveshaft`**: Centre driveshaft between the pinions of the two bulkheads, with the fixed metal pins of the cup joints, STEP, STL and parameter file. The cup joints, the rear pinion shaft with the spur gear, the motor mount with its cap and the diff outdrives are stock parts as well (not part of the speedrun design) and are shown in the assembly picture only. Status of all these parts: not verified. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#centre-driveshaft-stock-parts-not-part-of-the-speedrun-design).
