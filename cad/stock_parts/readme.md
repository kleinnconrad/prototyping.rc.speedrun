# Stock parts of the Carten T410R
The files in this directory are models of stock parts of the Carten T410R. They are not part of the speedrun design and are not manufactured in this project. They are modelled for the completeness of the build: the assembly of the speedrun chassis contains them, so that the space around the speedrun parts can be checked.

* **`/pivot_block`**: Pivot blocks of the wishbones, long block (1 piece) and short block (3 pieces), STEP, STL and parameter file. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#pivot-blocks-stock-parts-not-part-of-the-speedrun-design).
* **`/driveshaft`**: Centre driveshaft between the pinions of the two bulkheads, with cross holes for the metal pins of the cup joints, STEP, STL and parameter file. The metal pins, the cups on the pinions and the diff outdrives are shown in the assembly picture only. Description and checks: see the [CAD readme](../readme.md#centre-driveshaft-stock-parts-not-part-of-the-speedrun-design).
